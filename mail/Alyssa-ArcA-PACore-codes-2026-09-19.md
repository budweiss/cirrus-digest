Quick follow-up while you're looking over the Arc A email — no need to

In the first email I flagged that I was citing PA Core standard *families*
(CC.1.1.4, CC.1.3.4, CC.1.4.4) rather than exact sub-letter codes, since
Great Minds doesn't print PA-specific codes in the Teach Book itself and I
didn't want to guess a letter. Turns out Great Minds publishes their own
official PA Core correlation guide for this exact curriculum (Level 4) —
found it on their site, not guessed or generated:

https://greatminds.org/hubfs/Alignment_Studies_AL_2024/Pennsylvania/AL_PA_Level_4_Alignment_Guide.pdf

Cross-checked every Arc A lesson against it directly. Here are the exact
codes, replacing the family-level guesses from the first email:

**Lesson 1 (pp. 14-19):** No standard — the guide doesn't code this lesson
at all, confirming it's a pure activation/hook lesson, not content-bearing.

**Lesson 2 (pp. 20-31):** CC.1.1.4.D (phonics/word analysis), CC.1.1.4.E
(fluency — accuracy), CC.1.3.4.K (reads grade-level literary fiction).
Correction from the first email: the Notice-and-Wonder work itself isn't
tied to a specific literary-analysis code in the guide — it falls under the
general reading-range standard (K), not a distinct "observation" standard.

**Lesson 3 (pp. 32-45):** CC.1.1.4.D, CC.1.1.4.E, CC.1.3.4.B (cites details,
makes inferences), CC.1.3.4.E (structural elements of poems/prose — the
Poem Organizer work), CC.1.3.4.I (vocabulary strategies), CC.1.3.4.K.

**Lesson 4 (pp. 46-57):** CC.1.1.4.D, CC.1.1.4.E, CC.1.3.4.B, CC.1.3.4.E,
**CC.1.3.4.F** (figurative language), CC.1.3.4.I, CC.1.3.4.K. The guide
confirms this exactly — Lesson 4 is the first lesson listed under
CC.1.3.4.F in the whole module, which matches the figurative/literal
"heart" pivot precisely.

**Lesson 5 (pp. 58-69):** CC.1.1.4.E (fluency — rate; phonics standard D
does not apply here), **CC.1.3.4.A** (determines theme from details),
CC.1.3.4.B, CC.1.3.4.I, CC.1.3.4.K. Again an exact match — Lesson 5 is the
first lesson listed under CC.1.3.4.A, confirming the central-idea work.

**Lesson 6 (pp. 70-77):** CC.1.1.4.E, CC.1.3.4.I, CC.1.3.4.K, and
**CC.1.4.4.F** for the writing task. One real correction here: the first
email cited the general CC.1.4.4 informative-writing standard for the
"write complete sentences" task, but the guide actually codes Lesson 6
under **conventions of language (F)**, not the informative-writing-content
standards (which the guide doesn't apply until Lesson 11). So Lesson 6 is a
conventions checkpoint, not yet a full informative-writing standard — a
useful distinction if this goes into a formal document.

The guide covers foundational skills, reading (informational + literature),
writing, speaking/listening, and language, lesson-by-lesson, for all four
Level 4 modules — so it's a good one to keep on hand for Arcs B-E too, and
you're welcome to look up codes yourself against it any time rather than
waiting on the next arc's write-up.

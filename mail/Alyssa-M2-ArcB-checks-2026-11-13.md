LESSON 8 — Wonder
Focus: noticing and wondering about The Invention of Hugo Cabret, and the story elements of the Writing Model for Module 2

1. (vocab) A climax is:
A. the first sentence of a story
B. a list of the characters
C. the title page of a book
D. the point in a story at which characters face their most important problem

2. (target) Which story elements does a narrative include?
A. a thesis and three reasons
B. setting, characters, problem, events, and resolution
C. only a title and an author
D. a table of contents and an index

3. (target) In "The Girl, the Goat, and the Fortune," what is the problem?
A. The goat is lost in a big city.
B. The soldier's helmet has fallen over the edge of a hillside.
C. The army has no horses.
D. The girl cannot find her house.

4. (target) Why is the Narrative Writing Planner shaped like a mountain?
A. The planner is a map of Greece.
B. Mountains are easy to draw.
C. A story builds up to an exciting high point, the climax, and then comes down to the resolution.
D. Every story takes place on a mountain.

Hi Alyssa,

An update from the weekly district check: for the first time, we found a Pennsylvania district that has written some of its own Arts & Letters material, and it covers your module.

Parkland School District (Lehigh County) adopted Arts & Letters for its elementary grades in June 2025. Its curriculum page now has a short overview, written by the district, for every K-5 module, including Grade 4 Module 1, A Great Heart:

https://www.parklandsd.org/fs/resource-manager/view/9cbc85fb-6bf2-4477-bffc-2f96d5063c45

The page with every grade and module, plus a recording of their October 2025 parent night, is here:

https://www.parklandsd.org/departments/office-of-teaching-and-learning/k-12-curriculum

To be upfront about what it is: it's written for parents, not teachers. It has no pacing, no lesson-by-lesson plan and no PA Core codes, so it doesn't replace or change anything we've sent you. Two parts might still be a useful comparison for your review:

- Vocabulary: a 12-word list that students are expected to know "for their tests." It mixes the science words (circulatory system, coronary, blood vessel) with the module's big ideas (literal, figurative, greathearted, empathetic).
- Writing goals: the district's checklist for the module's informative essay. It asks for a hook, a thesis with two supporting points, two proof paragraphs with evidence and elaboration, and a conclusion that answers "so what?" That could be a useful outside check against the success criteria in the Module Finale lessons.

The rest lists the texts, poems, paintings and videos, plus some at-home activities, all of which you'll already know from the Teach Book.

We also checked four more districts using Arts & Letters (three in Michigan, one in Massachusetts) and Utah's statewide approval. None of them publish anything of their own yet. We'll keep checking weekly and let you know if anything more teacher-facing turns up.

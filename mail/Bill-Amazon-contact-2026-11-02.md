Hi Bill,

I could not find a named person at Amazon who handles landscaping and snow removal, and Amazon does not appear to publish one. The route I would use has two parts:

1. Register directly with Amazon. Its supplier site is https://supply.amazon.com/ (Supplier Connect). Amazon says its procurement team may reach out when a business opportunity arises, so registering does not guarantee work. Amazon also recognizes veteran-owned and service-disabled veteran-owned status from the VA, the SBA, NVBDC and NaVOBA; the list is at https://www.amazonsdi.com/global.

2. Approach a contractor already serving Amazon sites. Facility work is often run through outside companies. East Coast Facilities, for example, advertises self-performed snow, landscaping and parking lot services nationwide and says it supports Amazon, so it may be worth asking about subcontracting. That is the company's own claim; I have not confirmed it with Amazon.

If you have particular Amazon buildings in mind, send me the locations and I will look for who handles those sites.

Amazon doesn't publish a named contact for snow and landscaping. Two roles handle it:

1. The RME (Reliability and Maintenance Engineering) manager at each Amazon building runs that site's facilities. On LinkedIn, search: Amazon RME Manager Middletown Delaware (or the town of the building you want).
2. Amazon's procurement team has Category Managers for Facility Management Services, who choose facility-services vendors. On LinkedIn, search: Amazon Category Manager Facility Management Services.

THE BIGGER THING

While tracking that down I found the source I told you back in July I would go
looking for, and it is better than I expected. New Castle County publishes a
directory of all 224 community associations in the county, each with a named
officer or management company. It is free and public.

  - 189 are self-managed — no management company. Those are your prospects.
  - 35 are already held by a management company. That is your competition,

    Valley 5, Mastriana 4, and nobody else more than 2.

  - 13 are in Middletown, and every one of those 13 is self-managed.

For what it is worth, Knight is already in that directory yourself, on Enclave
at Odessa.

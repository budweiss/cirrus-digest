Hi Bill,

A correction on Monday's development-leads email, and an apology for the noise.

That message said 504 new leads had appeared in a week. They hadn't. The state
data source we pull from renumbered its records, and our weekly comparison read
every one of them as brand new. Most of what you saw were communities built
years ago — some as far back as 2004 — not new developments.

The leads themselves weren't wrong. The workbook attached to that email is
accurate; it was the "504 new" count and the highlighted list at the top that
were meaningless.

We've fixed it at the root rather than filtering the symptom. Those records are
now tracked by their own permanent project ID instead of the one the source
reissues, so a renumbering can't be mistaken for news again. We've also added a
ceiling — if a future run ever claims an implausible number of new leads, it
stops and flags it to us instead of emailing you.

While looking into it we found a second problem worth telling you about, because
it went the other way: the feed had been quietly missing recent records. The
source spells county names two different ways and our filter only recognised one
of them, which silently dropped 41 projects — 36 of them from 2023 onward, so
essentially all the recent ones. Those are now included, and they'll show up in
the normal weekly flow from here.

Nothing needed from you. Monday's email will be back to a real count, and if
there's genuinely nothing new that week, you won't get one at all.

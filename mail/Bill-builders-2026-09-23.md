What's in the workbook:

  Building Communities (30 builders). Each has at least one active new-home
  community in Delaware right now, and the communities are named on each row.
  It covers the national builders (Lennar, Ryan Homes, K. Hovnanian,
  D.R. Horton, Beazer, DRB) and the local ones (Schell Brothers, Insight,
  Ashburn, Benchmark and others).

  Not Building Communities (29 builders). These are custom, build-on-your-lot,
  modular and multifamily builders with no active community right now.

  Other Licensed - Unverified (442). These businesses hold a current Delaware
  contractor or developer license and have "Homes" or "Builders" in the name.
  They haven't been checked one by one, and some will be remodelers. They're
  included because you asked for every builder, and this is as close as the
  public records get.

Each row has the company, a contact and a mailing address. Where a builder
publishes who runs it, the label goes to that person. Where it doesn't
(K. Hovnanian and three others), the label reads "Attn: Division President" or
similar.

For labels, each tab opens directly in Word's mail merge (Mailings > Labels >
Use an Existing List). The last tab, "About This List," has the steps and says
where each address and name came from.

One limit worth knowing: Delaware doesn't license "home builders" as a separate
category, so no official complete list exists. The first two tabs are checked;
the third is the wide net.

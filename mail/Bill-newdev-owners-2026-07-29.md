I've updated the workbook to answer your main question. Each lead now shows the **Owner / Builder of record** — the entity that owns the parcel, usually the developer's LLC — and that owner's **mailing address**, pulled from the Kent and Sussex county assessor records. A few from the top of the list: Red Stone Ranch LLC, Dev Developments LLC, Fieldstone Village Apartments LLC, Salt Air Homes LLC, and Luther Village of Dover.

We matched an owner to about 150 of the leads. Where the Owner column is blank, it's usually because the development's original parcel has since been split into new lot numbers — in those cases the Parcel ID column lets you pull the current owner in one click at the county site (pride.kentcountyde.gov for Kent, map.sussexcountyde.gov for Sussex).

For a direct human contact, the owner's mailing address is the first route; for the engineer/firm behind a project, the "PLUS Communities" tab lists the project engineer and links to each project's full state application, which usually names the developer's contact.

We'll keep watching weekly and send you any new ones with the builder already attached.

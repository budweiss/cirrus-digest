Hi Justin,

The dashboard is up:

https://halftime.cirrustask.com

Signing in: enter this email address and it will send you a six-digit code.
There is no password and no account to create. The session lasts 30 days, so
you should rarely see the code screen after the first time.

What is on it now. Every home game, including the Paris game — flagged as not a
booking opportunity rather than quietly left out. Each game has two separate
columns, because they are two different kinds of option:

  Routing through  — acts with an announced show within three days of kickoff
                     and inside 200 miles. They are already in the area.
  Credit list      — acts known to have played a halftime somewhere. No tour to
                     track, so it applies to every date and is listed once.

A few things you will see that are worth knowing are deliberate.

Every panel states its own coverage — how many metros were searched and what
came back. So when a date looks thin, you can see whether it is thin because
nothing is out there or because we have not looked yet. Those are different
answers and the page never blurs them. Week 16 says "not searched" because the
league has not set the date.

December 20 is currently empty on the routing side. That is a real result,
not a gap: seven metros searched, nothing announced within three days. It will
change as dates are announced, and the sweep re-runs weekly.

Two that stand out. Olivia Rodrigo plays PPG Paints Arena two days after the
September 27 home game, and Dan + Shay play the same room two days before
October 11. Both are the kind of routing you asked about for staying relevant
with a younger crowd.

On 20 December — Styx have nothing announced after December 4, and their music
is already part of that stadium. Worth a conversation, though they would sit at
the top of the price range on the roster you sent rather than the $15-30k tier.

Where it is still thin, plainly: the credit list is small so far and grows
nightly, and the routing column will show you small-room acts alongside big
ones — each card says which, so you can skim past the 300-capacity club dates.

The three things from my last note would sharpen it a lot: a budget range for
the two target dates, which acts you have already used, and what a military tie
means to you for 1 November. Happy to be told the whole shape is wrong, too —
it is easier to change now than later.
